# Airport comparison writing spacing

Routine two-airport temperature comparisons wait 24 hours after a confirmed X
publication of that format before another writer attempt. This is editorial
pacing: a new pair or UTC date does not by itself make another comparison useful.
It does not change source qualification, required model checks, or other formats.

The gate uses the original confirmed receipt, its matching numeric X ID, and
the exact retained content/evidence binding. Missing, uncertain, conflicting,
legacy unbound, edited or future-dated records cannot establish that publication.
This is not a posting ceiling over unknown deliveries. Pending approvals and
previously saved drafts retain their existing sending rules.

The decision uses the operation's loaded publication state. It adds no shared
transaction, cross-writer lock or global spending cap; an external receipt not
yet visible in that state cannot establish spacing for this operation.

The source still reads and qualifies its observations. The runner reports the
editorial hold separately, and queued candidates are checked before selection
caps and again before writing. Such holds consume no writer slot and do not
become source failures, model verdicts or negative-cache hits. At exactly 24 hours
the ordinary checks can reconsider the format. No new request, retry, model,
prompt, refill, spending allowance or publishing permission is added.
