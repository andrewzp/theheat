## Completion gates

- Phase 0: truthful operations; publication containment; protected retention; canonical places; strict evidence/time checks; adversarial fixtures from real errors.
- Phase 1: one authenticated mutation/publishing authority; idempotent import; durable observations/jobs/outbox; backup restore; platform outcome reconciliation.
- Phase 2: measured global coverage and scientific supply, qualified observed-source pilots, event clustering and evidence-backed story discovery.
- Phase 3: unified editorial engine and review desk, validity-aware alternatives, traceable published outcomes, held-out editorial evaluation.
- Phase 4: post-age outcomes, verified media, reconciled costs, bounded experiments, weekly learning process and retired obsolete paths.

External account access or infrastructure decisions may limit particular packages, but do not close unrelated work. Do not activate automatic publishing until the assessed release gates pass. Public corrections and account spending require the separate authorization specified in the assessment.
