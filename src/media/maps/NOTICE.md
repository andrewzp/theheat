The derived JSON preserves all finite WGS84 shapefile polygon rings and their
source vertex order without simplification. Rendering uses neutral outlines only,
clipped to the locator; no administrative borders or physical-fire footprints.
Coarse geography cannot establish exact land/water or local position. Coastlines
are omitted in close-ups. Original archive and offline converter are retained.
